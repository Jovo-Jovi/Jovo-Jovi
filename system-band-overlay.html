Auto-align, from the pixels

The frames are found by reading the image, not by guessing. Press Detect. Drag only if a frame needs a nudge afterwards.

Square frames cannot fit slabs drawn in perspective. The render was generated from a prompt that said "receding very slightly", so the slabs are trapezoids. A CSS border is always an axis-aligned rectangle. Drag Depth to tilt all five toward a shared vanishing point, then drag a frame's green handle to fine-tune that one.

Four edge handles per frame. Top, bottom, left, right move independently — the corner-only handle could never fix a bottom edge on its own. Bottom detection also stops at the slab base now instead of running into its floor reflection.

Click mode is the one that works. Press it, then click the centre of each slab left to right. Each click snaps to the strongest edge either side of it, and to the top and bottom of that column. Five clicks, pixel-accurate, and it cannot mis-pair.

Auto detect looks for the ten frame edges and pairs them into five slabs. It reports a gap ratio and width ratio, and tells you when the pairing is implausible instead of handing you a wrong answer. The graph is the edge profile — green marks are detected edges.
