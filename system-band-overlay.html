Goal B · system band

Auto-align, from the pixels

The frames are found by reading the image, not by guessing. Press Detect. Drag only if a frame needs a nudge afterwards.

How it finds them. The image goes to a canvas, and every column's mean luminance across the middle band becomes a profile. The five slabs are the five brightest plateaus in that profile — the graph under the image is that data, with the detected bands marked. For each band it then runs a row profile to find the top and bottom edges. If the render has five brighter-than-background verticals, it will find them.

If Detect fails, raise or lower Sensitivity and press it again. The profile graph shows you what it's seeing, so you can tell whether the threshold is cutting through the slabs or through the background.
