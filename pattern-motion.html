Goal B · card panel pattern motion

Six patterns. Pick one per card.

Pure CSS. No images, no SVG, no JavaScript, nothing to generate — the entire system is about 2.5 KB of stylesheet for all seven cards combined. Every pattern reads as instrumentation rather than decoration, which is the register your page is already in.

Why this needed no assets. I had been treating "pattern motion" as feature-aligned technical overlays — a packet on the rail drawn in that render, a pulse on the verification dot in that one. That needs the artwork and it needs seven bespoke SVGs. A repeating pattern over the image needs neither. It works on any render, including ones you haven't made yet.
