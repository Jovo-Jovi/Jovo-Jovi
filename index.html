Front to back

Precision on both sides of the stack.

I trained as a mechatronics engineer, where a system either works under real load or it does not. That standard came with me into software, and it is why I specify before I build.

Nothing reaches the data without passing validation and a per-row permission check. That is the part of the design I spend the most time on.

Arabic first, not translated

Full right-to-left layout with a bilingual toggle, designed from the first wireframe rather than retrofitted onto an English page.

Permissions designed, not patched

Row-level security enabled and default-deny on every table, with four roles separated at the database rather than in application code.

Specified before built

Business rules catalogued and numbered, the data contract frozen before a page is drawn, and a reviewer pipeline checking each phase against those documents.
